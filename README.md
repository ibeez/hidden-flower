# The Hidden Flower

Fig. 05 in my "math facts that live in my head" series. Four Talavera tiles slide together one at a time, and where four corners meet, a whole new flower appears that no single tile has: 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 1.

Open it: https://ibeez.github.io/hidden-flower/

Drag a tile out and let go to watch it snap home. Tap the fractions at the top to replay. Add `?loop` to see the full loop with the wall of flowers.

## Sound

All synthesized with Web Audio, silent until your first touch, with a `sound` button to mute it. A fingerpicked nylon guitar in D with a soft heartbeat bass loops every 14.4 seconds. Each tile lands with a ceramic clack, the new flower blooms with a harp sparkle, the wall of flowers gets a bigger sparkle, and the scatter whooshes. A tile you drag home lands with a wooden tock. Notes for other pieces: [`../chiptune-music.md`](../chiptune-music.md).
